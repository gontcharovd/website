Yes, I'm heavily biased working at Databricks, but Genie is absolutely amazing.

I was following up with a prospect working at a large MedTech company who asked for the following:

* What projects does Databricks already do at our company in Supply Chain and Procurement?
* Who are you working with?
* What is the measurable impact?

It's Friday late afternoon and most colleagues have logged off for the week - where do I get this info?

I forwarded his email to Genie and got a comprehensive list of use cases, people, and impact (including numbers) within minutes.

It even drafted the response email (which I had to mostly rewrite to make it shorter, with less fluff, and sound more human).

But the content was all there. The prospect now has a clear view of what we could do for his team.

Fingers crossed.
