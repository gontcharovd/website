I just listened to this [The AI for Sales Podcast](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6tdhsc9PTYAxVOqjO5f0cr?si=z3Ze17ZgRFyyXNK5DuVbzg) about why AI makes people worse at their jobs. It gave me several ideas for building agents that can support my work as a BDR at Databricks.

Specifically, based on my 9 months on the job so far, I believe the following agents could really support my work:

* An autonomous agent that crawls through my CRM and fixes any contact that has a bad phone number. A bad phone number means either a contact with no phone number, or a contact with phone numbers that have "could not connect - bad number" Outreach activities on them.
* A sales signal agent that looks at my call notes[^1] and external sources (news, reports, etc.) and notifies me when I should talk to someone about that particular thing.
* Call block follow-up agent. My call blocks are very focused: for 60 minutes I do nothing but smile and dial. I don't send follow-up emails, I don't log anything in my CRM[^2]. Instead, I log an Outreach task that specifies what I need to do (send an email with specific content, book a meeting, log info in my CRM). I currently do this myself after the call block. This admin time could potentially be fully outsourced to an agent.

Without a doubt, I'll come up with more ideas as I become better at building agents on Databricks.

[^1]: I already have a Databricks Job that compiles all my written call notes in Outreach into a Delta Table.
[^2]: That's how I achieve 40 dials per hour.
