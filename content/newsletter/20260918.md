I've been a BDR for over seven months now. By now I feel I have a good daily workflow for inbound and outbound prospecting, CRM admin, and other aspects of my job. I know how to influence my results and which metrics to track.

About three months ago I started playing with AI (Claude). My company offers me pretty much unlimited tokens, so I took the opportunity to try out many different things. Some of my experiments were a complete waste of time and productivity, others yielded results - mostly by saving me time.

Some concrete value I'm getting from AI today includes:

* Having completely automated my lead-scraping workflow[^1]
* Logging objects in the CRM (contacts, opportunities, etc.)
* Creating call tasks based on call-connects with hand-written notes.

Over the coming months I hope to write about how data and AI can improve my sales performance. I want to write to crystallize my thoughts and truly understand how analytics and AI can benefit salespeople.

[^1]: Scraping 20 leads with contact details into the CRM took me almost one hour per day in the past.
