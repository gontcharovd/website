I make about 200 calls per week resulting in 20-40 connects. For each connect, I make notes during the call where I write in key points that were discussed, objections raised, and next actions required. I also include any relevant information about the account.

This helps me connect with the same person afterward. On top of that, over time, these call notes result in a wealth of information about the account. The problem is that in Salesforce, it's hard to find the notes as they are mapped to a contact's activity history. Searching for relevant notes across contacts is really difficult.

What if I could design an agent that can traverse through all my notes and answer specific questions about this account?

Even better, once I have sufficient notes, I'd like to build a recommendation engine that continuously reads them, and recommends me on a daily basis who I should call that day during my prospecting blocks.

With Isaac (our internal Claude tool), I've already built part of the code. I did some dry runs, and the results are pretty remarkable. The LLMs handle translations[^1], multiple call result codes over time ("meeting booked", "meeting set", etc.), and manage to extract key information from my notes (relationships, Databricks products, competitor products, timelines, budgets, etc.)

The solution currently consists of delta tables and a terminal. Next, I'll build a Genie Agent on top that will act as a real time chat interface.

[^1]: I write in several languages.
