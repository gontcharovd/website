Before I joined Databricks I was a data consultant for manufacturing and energy. One platform I particularly enjoyed working with was the [United Manufacturing Hub](https://www.umh.app/?r=0) (UMH).

Yesterday, using Claude, I managed to in one evening:

1. Reinstall Proxmox on my spare Linux laptop.
2. Create a Ubuntu Server template that I can reuse.
3. Install the United Manufacturing Hub on a VM cloned from the template.
4. Play with a first data flow.

I must say, AI has come a long way. It seems that many parts of my work are significantly easier and/or faster now. While I have no intention of leaving my corporate sales job, I'll definitely enjoy playing with the UMH this winter.
