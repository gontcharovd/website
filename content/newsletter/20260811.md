I'm definitely more impressed with Genie now. While creating two pie charts on my weekly call analytics dashboard, Genie figured out that we need to join *both* calls to leads as well as contacts.

When designing the dataset for the dashboard myself, I only thought of contacts. I wondered why Genie created a `leads` dataset, thinking it was a mistake.

Genie calmly explained that to get my total calls, we need to look at calls I made to both leads and contacts. I don't often call leads (because I always convert them to contacts), but other users may do so.

Good catch.
