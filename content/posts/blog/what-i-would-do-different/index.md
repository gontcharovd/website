I'm now five months into my tech sales job at Databricks, working as a Business Development Representative (BDR). While I'm satisfied with my current gig, I still like to compare it to my previous work as a data engineering contractor. I'm a big believer in self-reflection.

Knowing what I do today, what would I have done differently?

First, I'd look at the reason why I decided to leave that gig: I felt stuck in a rut of contracting, being at the mercy of my recruiter. I wasn't able to reliably drum up new business

Mistakes I made
* Procrastinating my studying irrelevant technologies (Scala, etc.)
* Not prioritizing work: daily study hour instead of prospecting or marketing
* Not focusing on the present work: being unsatisfied with my current project and dreaming of the next one 
* Comparing myself to more established more technical tech people 

What I learned at Databricks so far:
* Evaluating if my work has impact
* Ruthless prioritization
* Learning that I can't make time for everything (4000 weeks)
* Finishing work I don't like by just getting started without philosophising about it
* Qualification: dismissing leads that are not good opportunities.
